# G0-K: Beyond planar scalar collapse

## Audit finding
The current G0-D/F biological surrogate implements scalar activities on a periodic 2D Euclidean grid. The existing G0-E gauge module uses fixed 2×2 matrices. Neither implements a polycosmic brain. Biochemical fields can legitimately live on a spatial mesh; that **does not** justify compressing the organism's internal cognition into a flat scalar field.

## Distinct carriers
- **Physical substrate**: a possibly curved tissue manifold with chemistry, diffusion, mechanics. The 2D mesh remains an inexpensive numerical *chart*, not a claim of dimensionality of mind.
- **Worldlet fiber**: a local typed state space E_i, carrying metric or bilinear form H_i, algebra A_i, observer and event clock; nonidentical dimensionalities may need rectangular correspondences rather than invertible gauge equivalences.
- **Tendril**: a directional map E_j→E_i with typed composition and an explicitly reported kernel/cokernel or round-trip remainder when not invertible.
- **Loop holonomy**: conjugates under frame changes; its similarity invariants survive. No single loop is an organism's identity.
- **Developmental dynamics**: chemical and biomechanical constraints generate plausible physical contacts, not semantic proof.
- **Neuromorphic computation**: quiescent exact decay and message-triggered updates; realized efficiency must be measured, never inferred from sparse event count.

## Concrete algebra instantiated
`esmf/geometry_k.py` implements GL(n) covariance of transported bilinear mismatch and 2+1 Lorentz hyperboloid geometry. The hyperboloid is **a test geometry**, not a claim that the brain literally inhabits Lorentzian spacetime. Positive hyperbolic distance belongs to the upper sheet while the ambient bilinear form is indefinite. Consequently the edge 'mismatch energy' with an indefinite metric may be negative and **must not** be inserted as a dissipative Lyapunov function.

For local change of frames B_i, one has u_i' = B_i u_i, U_ij' = B_i U_ij B_j^-1, H_i' = B_i^-T H_i B_i^-1. Bilinear mismatch is invariant for any invertible B_i,B_j, regardless of whether U preserves the metric. Loop holonomy transforms by conjugation. We test these identities numerically, not prove them in Lean.

## Anti-ornament conditions
A higher algebra, non-Euclidean manifold, conjugate field, or additional cognitive organ is admissible only when it supplies a novel operator, invariant, falsifiable performance difference, or faithful transport remainder. Matched Euclidean/real, positive-metric, and no-holonomy baselines are mandatory.
