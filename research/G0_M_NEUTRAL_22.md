# G0-M · Neutral (2,2) fibers without planar collapse

User intent: a geometric carrier of neutral signature (2,2), rather than a two-dimensional Euclidean plane. **Notation discipline:** `GF(2,2)` commonly suggests Galois-field notation, whereas the present prototype is a real four-dimensional vector space with bilinear form signature (2,2). We use `R^(2,2)`, `O(2,2)`, or neutral-signature fiber, pending precise definition of any intended `GF(2,2)` theory.

## First discriminating properties
- Two positive and two negative directions; the nonzero null cone exists.
- A nonzero null vector can have quadratic form zero. This is not a Euclidean norm.
- Transformations preserving the bilinear form can mix positive and negative directions without changing the form.
- Lossy 4→3 transport may have negative, zero, or positive signed remainder magnitude, and thus requires a separate positive-definite cost instrument for budgets and dissipation.
- Gauge transformations of local metrics preserve signed mismatch when all objects transform consistently, not when coordinates are merely relabeled.

## Explicit warning
An O(2,2) invariant indefinite form cannot simultaneously serve as a nonnegative dissipative energy for unrestricted fiber states. Any claim of stability requires a separate positive metric, cone restriction, or a provable coercive functional.
