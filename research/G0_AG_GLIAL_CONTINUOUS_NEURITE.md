# G0-AG — Continuous Quaternionic Neurites and a Glial Support Worldlet

**Evidence floor:** S/H for biology, endogenous semantics, and proposed advantages. P (conditional, standard mathematics) for quaternion identities, left-gauge covariance, and exact proxy bookkeeping. Executable tests are *contract verification*, not M held-out evidence. No claim of quantum coherence, organismal cognition, or biologically realistic glial physiology.

## Its own telos

ESMF-0 is not trying to maximize dimension or choose an impressive algebra. A living analogue must preserve viable opportunities to sense, act, maintain traces, and construct *revisable* distinctions. Only the relationships that survive intervention, local material cost, and representational transport have grounds to become anatomy. G0-AF made the neurite itself a paid expansion of representation. G0-AG asks whether an additional *supporting tissue compartment* can regulate that expansion, and whether the connection along the neurite can itself be revised continuously without rewriting its past.

This is a **separate, additive bounded generator** over G0-AE/G0-AF. No previous experiment, benchmark or frozen historical result is modified. The existing limited two-context observation environment remains, as do AF's synthetic ATP and chemical packets; no endogenous semantic-context discovery or physical nervous tissue is claimed.

## Typed, simultaneously evolving state

Let \(r_i\) be the number of paid neurite segments for cell \(i\). Its typed carrier is

\[
E_i(r_i)=\operatorname{Mat}_{2}(\mathbb R)\times
          \mathbb H^{\,r_i+1}.
\]

The first factor retains the neutral (2,2) determinant form and chart seam of G0-AF. Its quadratic form is **not positive energy**. The second factor now contains *two compatible but distinct experimental representations*:

- The inherited **discrete** \(Q_8\) phase per soma/segment, which G0-AF still uses for tissue tip bias.
- A new **continuous** unit quaternion \(q_{i,k}\in S^3\subset\mathbb H\) per soma/segment, and a unit quaternion \(U_{i,k}\) per directed segment transport.

These are not an arbitrary learned higher-dimensional manifold, a hypercomplex quantum state, a local octonion field, or a model of human consciousness. Each quaternion is four real coordinates; the real carrier dimension is \(4+4(r_i+1)\). The two-dimensional or three-dimensional extracellular voxel coordinates are *physical substrate discretizations*, not a bound on this cognitive fiber dimension.

A paid extension adds both a new phase and a new edge-transport unit. Starting from the old tip \(q\), a unit quaternion \(R\) determined by growth direction initializes

\[
q_{\rm new}=Rq,\qquad
U_{\rm new}=q_{\rm new}\bar q.
\]

It follows algebraically that \(U_{\rm new}q=q_{\rm new}\) (initial zero transport defect). That is an initialization identity, **not learned invariant meaning**.

A paid retraction removes both new objects and appends their identities to typed \`[R]_transport_continuous\` provenance. No lost section is recovered silently from a quaternion scalar.

## A real gauge-aware transport obligation

Define the positive **auxiliary** discrepancy

\[
d_U(q_s,q_t)=\|q_t-Uq_s\|^2_{\mathbb R^4}.
\]

For independent local unit-quaternion frames \(g_s,g_t\), set

\[
q'_s=g_sq_s,\quad q'_t=g_tq_t,\quad
U'=g_t U g_s^{-1}.
\]

Then \(d_{U'}(q'_s,q'_t)=d_U(q_s,q_t)\) exactly. This follows from the multiplicativity of quaternion norm and the unit condition. It provides an explicit and testable relation-preservation target rather than a decorative quaternionic encoding.

A locally observed four-coordinate target from G0-AE can trigger a *paid* experience update to the tip phase. The older \(U\) is left unchanged, exposing a transport mismatch; a **separate** paid connection-repair event may later adopt \(U_{\rm repaired}=q_t\bar q_s\). It retains the replaced operator and observation reference as \`[R]_transport_prior\` in a bounded audit record. That repaired link is algebraically fitted to its two endpoints, which is not evidence that it transports semantics correctly to unseen contexts.

Normalized linear interpolation is used as a modest \(S^3\) update operator. The antipodal zero case is rejected, not silently clipped or projected. This operation is left-gauge equivariant when the two quaternions share the same frame. Do not infer it is gauge equivariant for distinct endpoints without applying the separate connection operator.

## Glial support is constitutive, optional, and accounted for

G0-AG introduces a local resource-and-signaling Worldlet, *astrocyte-inspired* but not biologically equivalent. It carries a fixed voxel, substrate, transferable proxy, calcium-like gate, and an optional tripartite state. All operations are separate transition channels in the **same joint finite continuous-time generator** as the AF tissue and AE cognitive channels.

It can take external resources; convert substrate to a transferable proxy; gate calcium-like activity in response to observed neuronal activity or already existing contact; transfer its resource to a nearby cell that has storage capacity; emit a local guidance packet; and, given a paid neuron–neuron contact, enter or leave a *paid three-party support state*.

The three-party state is a genuine ternary conditional modifier in this chosen state space: it alters both contact-release and cognitive-encounter rates. This does **not** establish irreducibility relative to a richer hidden-variable pairwise model. Glial support is **not mandatory**: the neurons retain their own resource supply and can still sense or grow without glial transfer when supported by their independent substrate.

An important counterworld comes from actual neurobiology. A 2026 *Nature Communications* study of astrocytic PI3Kα links glial metabolic signaling, serine availability, hippocampal plasticity and memory in mice (Fernández-Rodrigo et al., DOI [10.1038/s41467-026-77319-0](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-77319-0)). A 2025 study in glucose-supplied rat/mouse hippocampal preparations found MCT1/MCT2 lactate transport was **not required for synchronized transmission** under its tested conditions (Söder et al., DOI [10.1111/jnc.70251](https://doi.org/10.1111/jnc.70251)). These are distinct questions and models; neither proves our glial proxy law. 3D reconstructions of perisynaptic astrocytic processes and ER microdomain variation (Denizot et al., DOI [10.1002/glia.70091](https://doi.org/10.1002/glia.70091)) motivate local structural heterogeneity, not a quaternionic cellular mechanism.

## Conservation and simultaneous law

Let \(E_{\rm AF}\) include neuron ATP proxy, substrate, extracellular chemical packets, and the AF waste ledger. Let \(E_{\rm glia}\) include glial substrate, transferable pool and its waste. The initial combined account is \(8+3=11\), in discrete synthetic units.

AF records all nutrient entries into its subsystem, including those transferred internally from glia. Define \(N_{\rm AF}\) as that AF input ledger, \(N_{\rm glia}\) as external glial input, and \(T\) as recorded glia-to-AF transfer. Then:

\[
E_{\rm AF}+E_{\rm glia}
=11+N_{\rm AF}+N_{\rm glia}-T.
\]

The extra \(T\) subtraction prevents accounting for one internally transferred unit as an external source to the **combined** organism. Equivalently, its external inputs are \((N_{\rm AF}-T)+N_{\rm glia}\). This is exact under the **chosen token conversion rules**; it is not an experimentally measured biological energy conservation law. G0-AF's separate structural material ledger remains unchanged.

A transition is accepted only if the resulting state satisfies both the base AF ledger and this combined ledger; no module may spend tissue ATP it does not have. A glial transfer does not create ATP for the neuron; it supplies substrate for its still-costly conversion.

The dynamics are defined by a joint generator

\[
(\mathcal L f)(s)=\sum_a r_a(s)\,[f(F_a(s))-f(s)]
\]

with state-dependent rates for all AF, AE, glial and continuous transport events. Some hazards do not commute as interventions: replenishment can enable subsequent sensing, whereas sensing is blocked by resource exhaustion. Gillespie event order is just a stochastic **realization** of the coupled law, not a sequential brain ontology.

## Explicit gaps and adversarial controls

The current laboratory still has just **two neural cells and one glial compartment**. It has neither a realistic neurite cytoskeleton nor a continuous three-dimensional force field. Continuous quaternion transports are coded but not yet measured on held-out tasks. In addition, the glial cell is at a fixed voxel, calcium is two bounded integer units, and AF's inherited Q8 phase remains a separate state: **the continuous transport does not yet determine neurite growth direction**. The new feedback is limited to observed experience/transport repair; stronger continuous-phase-to-growth coupling is a separately testable hypothesis, not an accomplished integration.

The glial state and resource transactions are *not* a demonstration of a physiological lactate shuttle. Its effects could plausibly be replicated by a resource-aware conventional controller.

A matched-control experiment must include: glia-on/off with equal total external nutrient budgets; matched local resource pools with no ternary contact modifier; Q8-only, continuous-quaternion, algebraically identical real-valued, shuffled-connection and fixed-topology variants; a hidden-state pairwise controller capable of mimicking three-party effects; and a matched observer trained on the same history, seeds, wall time and memory. A naive unregulated or underfunded control is not meaningful.

**Kill rule:** If the glia-augmented model does not improve held-out resilience, metabolic viability, transport stability or useful distinctions at comparable resource/compute budgets, retire the claimed glial advantage. If equal-information real implementations tie, retire any exclusive hypercomplex computational advantage. If repaired connections fit only observed data but fail under new frames or contexts, do not promote semantic-transport claims.
