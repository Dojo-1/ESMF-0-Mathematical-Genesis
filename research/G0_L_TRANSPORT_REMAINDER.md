# G0-L · Loss-aware translation between heterogeneous Worldlets

A Worldlet may have a dimension, metric, clock and named representation kind distinct from another Worldlet. A correspondence consists of **two declared maps**, not an assumed inverse: T:E_i→E_j and R:E_j→E_i. Round-trip remainder is r_i(x)=x−RTx.

Under independent basis changes B_i and B_j, T'=B_j T B_i^-1, R'=B_i R B_j^-1, and r_i'(B_i x)=B_i r_i(x). A compatible positive metric H_i'=B_i^-T H_i B_i^-1 preserves r_i^T H_i r_i. The raw coordinate length does not.

**Mathematical obstruction:** when dim(E_j)<dim(E_i), every linear T has a nontrivial kernel. Therefore no linear reconstruction R can satisfy RT=I on all of E_i. This is a rank-nullity theorem consequence, not a claim that semantic compression is impossible on constrained lower-dimensional data.

**Non-scalar warning:** the implemented magnitudes require a positive metric to be true norms; arbitrary indefinite metrics may produce negative squared magnitudes. Signed bilinear invariants remain available as separate typed diagnostics.

**No theorem transport:** no proof of cognition, no established hypercomplex field, no empirical energy gain. G0-L only repairs the carrier semantics that subsequent experiments must preserve.
