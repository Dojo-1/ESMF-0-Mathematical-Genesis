## What did emerge, and what failed

The **full** model averaged **49.17 growth events and 16.58 prunings**, reached 72 live directed contacts, and ended with a mean local learning-rate spread of **0.0855** and mean spread in three-component regulatory mixtures of **0.8151**. Fixed reference had no growth/pruning, zero rate and rule-mixture spread, and about 39.4 live contacts. All 12 paired streams preserved exact energy and material ledgers.

These are genuine **model dynamics** under weakly differentiated initial conditions, but are not necessarily meaningful biological or cognitive specialization. The local regulatory update rules are still selected from a fixed primitive basis, and the 3D tissue geometry is an abstract contact constraint. More contacts and greater regulatory differentiation were not useful on this task. The last world regime was the most harmful for the full model (mean Brier 0.278475 versus fixed 0.260691).

### An exact restriction to investigate before adding biology

[G0-AI P1](G0_AI_P1_SYMMETRY_OBSTRUCTION.md) proves a conditional sign-equivariance of the present code under globally inverted sensory histories and identical labels. Its odd latent features and affine logistic readout cannot express a nonconstant *even* target exactly over all mirrored input histories. The external delayed parity-like target is even under that involution. This is a mathematically specific **representation obstruction**; it is not proven to explain all of the observed Brier deficit on finite, asymmetric realized streams.

## Evidence decision

**R:** retire the claim that the particular G0-AI D0 growth and locally adaptive rule mixtures improve temporal prediction. This negative is supported by the paired synthetic development measurement, not a universal statement about morphogenesis or self-organization.

**P (conditional):** exact integer ledger conservation and the explicitly formulated sign-equivariance theorem, each under their software-model assumptions.

**A:** synthetic resource and neural/chemical analogues; implementation-defined graded observables, not biological gene regulatory networks.

**H:** a genuinely *compositional* local-observation algebra, with plastic creation and retirement of relation types under evidence and cost, might overcome a representational symmetry without defining a final anatomy. It still needs fair ordinary second-order and recurrent baselines, independent held-out tasks, and usable measurements.

The [Nature study on stimulation-linked assemblies and gene regulation](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10879-9) motivated this question; its human physiological findings do not confer biological legitimacy on this synthetic substrate.
