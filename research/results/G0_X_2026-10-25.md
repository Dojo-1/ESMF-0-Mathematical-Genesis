# G0-X · Learning an unknown Z₂ neutral monodromy from intervention traces
Date 2026-10-25

## Protocol and methods

[Pre-registered binary comparison](../G0_X_MONODROMY_PROTOCOL.md) preceded running any G0-X experiment. Over base M=S¹×R, each action trace has a net integer winding w. The associated neutral fiber Mat₂(R), Q(F)=det(F) of signature (2,2), is locally transported by physical Jacobians in SL₂. Candidate global transition at the angular seam is either identity I or transposition P:vec(F)→vec(F^T), with P^T H P=H, det P=−1.

The instrument first learns two physical causal coefficients from 140 short noisy trajectories using the already implemented G0-S learner. For each candidate seam, it predicts ten independently generated *noisy odd-winding fiber observations* and selects the candidate minimizing squared discrepancy. It then predicts 38 novel odd-winding cases, 18 zero-winding cases and 10 held-out **double-winding** cases. Sensor noise std .006 per fiber component except exact double-winding controls.

This is selection among two **supplied** hypotheses, not unrestricted discovery of topology, and training has privileged access to a faithful four-coordinate transported-frame sensor. A conventional real-valued model with the same binary parity gate and calibration can reproduce the predictions.

## Exploratory third-monodromy stress [M/H]

After the two-candidate primary experiment, a third neutral orientation-reversing isometry was added by swapping the two diagonal entries of F. This is also an O(2,2) operator with det=−1 but is **not the declared transposition matrix in the fixed sensor chart**. It may share the same underlying orientation class; classification of the topology alone should not be conflated with identifying the precise connection/gluing representative.

With an additional **posthoc** rejection rule (reject if both candidate mean training losses exceed .01), the model rejected the unmodeled seam in 32/32 cases. The lowest candidate loss ranged from 0.19413 to 0.92772 (mean 0.53829), far above the threshold. This is a toy out-of-family rejection demonstration. It is not a calibrated novelty detector; the threshold was chosen after the primary result and must not be treated as independently preregistered.

## Interpretation [evidence-preserving]

- Conditional P: a transpose seam is a neutral isometry with orientation-reversing determinant; two windings yield P²=I.
- M: a small fitted model can infer which of two known transport rules generated held-out synthetic fiber observations, or abstain under insufficient winding evidence.
- H: richer Worldlet ecosystems can infer evolving bundle geometry/topology from interaction, transfer meaning across it, and outperform matched nongraphic representations.

The result is not evidence of sentience, advanced conceptual learning, embodied neural tissue, or resource/energy savings. It does not establish a general fiber-bundle topology-learning algorithm; the environment and hypotheses use the same small generative family.

## Next substantive discriminator

Remove the privileged full-rank frame sensor. Present only partially observed body/organ channels with independent clocks, noise and delays. Let the model infer hidden causal state and transported meanings while choosing whether to construct or merge Worldlets. Compare against calibrated real recurrent, hierarchical state-space and gauge-aware baselines, all at matched compute/parameters and identical sensory information. Only then can an advantage be attributed to the geometry rather than to favorable simulation assumptions.

## A conditional information-theoretic consequence (not yet measured)

Let T∈{I,P} denote the seam monodromy, W the observed winding number, and Y the noisy fiber sensor observation. In this model, if W is even then P^W=I, so the two candidate observation distributions are identical given identical physical action parameters and noise laws:

`Law(Y|T=I,W even,actions,F0) = Law(Y|T=P,W even,actions,F0)`.

Consequently the **conditional mutual information** `I(T;Y | W even,actions,F0)=0` under those assumptions. No amount of repeated even-winding sensing can distinguish the two monodromies unless additional information changes the experiment. Informative odd-winding interventions become possible *experiments*, not just more data. This is a conditional mathematical consequence of the finite candidate model [P under assumptions], not a measured improvement in energy usage. A future active-sensing benchmark should compare expected information gain per measured energy with random and equally informed conventional experiment-selection baselines.
