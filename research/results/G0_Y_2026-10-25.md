# G0-Y · Hidden-fiber synergy and resource-bounded active sensing — 2026-10-25

## Correction of G0-X's interpretation [P conditional]

The prior claim that an even-winding sensor always conveys no orientation information omitted the conditioning **on a known initial fiber state**. For hidden D=F₀₁−F₁₀∼N(0,v), independent equiprobable T∈{+1,−1}, and scalar sensors

Y_o=T D+ε_o, Y_e=D+ε_e,

both separate mutual informations I(T;Y_o) and I(T;Y_e) are zero but I(T;Y_o,Y_e)>0 when v>0 and both noise variances are finite. Conditional covariance has off-diagonal T v, and the exact posterior odds for a single pair are:

log[p(T=+1|y_o,y_e)/p(T=−1|y_o,y_e)] = 2v y_o y_e / [v(σ_o²+σ_e²)+σ_o²σ_e²].

The observations must be about the SAME latent D. Re-sampling D independently changes the likelihood and can manufacture false gluing if ignored.

Repeated observations from only one parity remain nonidentifying for T under the symmetric prior. After an odd observation, an even observation can have strictly positive **conditional** mutual information, and vice versa. Thus the information value of a channel is context-dependent, and myopic information gain can be zero at the start even though a two-step experiment is informative.

The original conditional statement I(T;Y_even | known F0,actions)=0 remains valid; it must **not** be generalized to unknown F0 and accumulated correlated evidence.

## Tested surrogate [A]

`esmf/active_epistemics_y.py` implements an exact online two-component Gaussian mixture over both T and D, with shared hidden D across an episode, scalar noisy observations, an even/odd winding-parity probe menu, quiet/focus sensor modes, reserve-limited resource charges, stopping at 95% posterior confidence, and explicit abstention. It provides three abstractions: fast somatic query events, an enteric-like resource ledger, and autonomic-like sensing precision control. These are surrogate interfaces, **not** verified biological organs, electrochemical dynamics, or measured hardware energy.

The anticipatory policy uses numerical two-step expected information when individual information gain is zero, otherwise one-step information per resource. Baselines: greedy myopic stop, fixed quiet/focused alternation, random, odd-only/even-only, and an identically informed ordinary real Bayesian controller **which ties by construction**. Shared finite action budget, same noise law, paired hidden orientation classes and seeded latent contrasts.

## Cognitive architecture implication [S/H]

An epistemically valuable observation need not itself convey information about the hidden property; its value may be enabling a later observation. The useful computational primitive here is not a scalar sensory reading but **shared latent provenance plus a complementarity-aware temporal relation**.

But the simple focused alternating circuit has comparable or better performance and orders-of-magnitude lower Python planning time. This motivates a low-latency compiled sensorimotor reflex, a separate homeostatic resource organ, and a cortical-like auditor invoked only on failure or novelty. It does **not** validate a brain, real energy reduction, or semantic concepts.

## Open falsifiers / next

- Remove same-D identity and explicitly model independent latent replacement: false gluing may produce spurious confidence.
- Track provenance/identity belief alongside orientation rather than forcing every observation into one persistent Worldlet.
- Test cheap compiled policies with held-out seeds, sensor-model mismatch, and opportunity cost.
- Compare strongest ordinary Bayesian/state-space controller with identical inputs: our geometric notation adds **zero demonstrated predictive gain** over it.
- Replace fully synthetic parity probes with actual embodied delayed actions and thermodynamic resource flows, and measure CPU/GPU power before efficiency claims.
