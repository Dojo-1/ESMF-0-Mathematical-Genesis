## Numerical qualifications

The mass diagnostic presently calls the input integral 'source' and infers the reaction loss as input minus reacted-mass change; it is an accounting identity, not an independent mechanistic validation of chemical sinks. Include independent total-budget and spatial resolution checks.

The motility generator samples independent noise and caps increments componentwise; it is a bounded random walk, not a calibrated Langevin integrator. Resource costs are phenomenological; its scalar `resource` is not a chemically stoichiometric free-energy ledger. A concentration field is not by itself an experimentally justified guidance model.

The developmental surrogate is 2-D periodic, with simplified soma contacts, no explicit neurite curve length, dendritic branch trees, cytoskeletal mechanics, glial dynamics, neurite self-avoidance or membrane currents.

## Follow-up audit — 2026-10-27

The model now distinguishes substrate, ATP-like energy, and structural material.
Nutrient and material supply are explicit; substrate-to-ATP conversion has an
efficiency loss; maintenance, exploration, extension, and contact formation spend
ATP. Paid extensions consume structural material and add to the measured branch
length. Exploratory fluctuations are separately retained and cannot change anatomy.
The energy and material residuals are independently computed from cumulative input,
current pools, installed length, conversion losses, and ATP expenditure. This is
surrogate bookkeeping, not a physical energy or thermodynamic derivation.

The CLI now supports paired chemistry-on/off, chemotaxis-sign reversal,
nutrient-supply ablation, and random-navigation controls (`--suite`). The
fixed-physical-time `--continuum` run compares `dt` with `dt/2`, a 2x grid with a
CFL-stable `dt/4`, and no-flux with periodic boundaries. It records current mature
contacts, the fraction of edges reaching the persistence threshold, binned edge
lifespans (active edges are right-censored), validity, and chemical, energy, and
material residuals.
