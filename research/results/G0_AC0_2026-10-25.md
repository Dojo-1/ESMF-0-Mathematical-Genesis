## Architectural advance, precise meaning

G0-AB's online world model adapted a *one-dimensional observation quotient* of the neutral Mat₂(R) fiber. G0-AC0 now changes the **connection itself** over a truly rank-four neutral signature (2,2) fiber, using a chart-consistent, metric-compatible family of 1-forms over base M=S¹×R:

`A_t = a_t X dθ + b_t Y dz`,

where X=(E₁₂+E₂₁), Y=(E₁₃+E₃₁) in the Lie algebra o(2,2), with index origin zero. The generators commute with the fixed orientation-reversing global transition R=diag(−1,1,1,1) but not with each other. Curvature at each developmental time t is `F_t=a_t b_t[X,Y] dθ∧dz` on the spatial slice; no claim is made about a canonical connection over space×developmental time.

Each synthetic organism performs a short θ- or z-directed test intervention by injecting a known local fiber section and collecting **two scalar projections** of the transported output, observed with independent Gaussian noise σ=.02. Input and output local charts are independently sampled among three nonorthogonal, position-dependent valid frames from the G0-Q atlas. Sensor covectors are transformed contragrediently, so measured predictions are chart invariant. The learner is not given the full four-vector target. It IS given the injected local section, chart adapters and action direction; this is a privileged calibration laboratory, not autonomous developmental cognition.

Five model options have the SAME two physically learned coefficients (a,b):
1. ADAPTIVE: online recursive least squares (RLS) with background covariance inflation and a surprise-triggered temporary increase.
2. CONSTANT_FORGETTING: identical RLS observations with fixed process variance q=.01.
3. NO_FORGETTING: RLS with q=0.
4. FROZEN_AFTER_CALIBRATION: after first 12 probes, parameters stop updating.
5. EQUIVALENT_REAL: the identical adaptive RLS algorithm with no claim that neutral notation grants a separate computational mechanism; tied predictions by construction.

The adaptive variant's covariance/trigger state is extra controller machinery, even though physical trainable coefficients are matched. No efficiency benefit may be inferred without counting it.

## Frozen held-out experiment [M]

The original protocol was committed before implementation. Following a 3-seed smoke test (IDs 0,1,2), the holdout range **100–131** (32 seeds) and hyperparameters were recorded in a protocol appendix *before evaluation*. Each world has 60 calibration opportunities; t=0..11 are burn-in. At each later step, error is measured on an **independent, noise-free probe section** whose output was never used to update the model. Axis alternates θ and z; all models receive the same training observations and available information. Mean squared error is averaged over held-out probes t=12..59 and all seeds.

| SMOOTH_DRIFT | 0.00005729 | **0.00005459** | 0.00006527 | 0.00012430 |
| ABRUPT_STEP | 0.00009832 | **0.00006763** | 0.00017022 | 0.00037502 |

The EQUIVALENT_REAL comparator matches ADAPTIVE exactly. Thus the experiment shows changing connection parameters can be tracked under gauge-covariant partial observation, but does **not** demonstrate a neutral-geometry performance advantage over a matching conventional real state estimator.

### Seed-paired differences: adaptive minus stronger controls

- STATIC vs NO_FORGETTING: adaptive MSE **+8.56e−6** (95% paired bootstrap [+4.64e−6,+12.67e−6]). Simpler no-forgetting wins on stationary data.
- SMOOTH_DRIFT vs CONSTANT_FORGETTING: **+2.70e−6** ([−5.14e−6,+10.90e−6]), inconclusive.
- ABRUPT_STEP vs CONSTANT_FORGETTING: **+30.69e−6** ([+18.67e−6,+43.04e−6]). Simple fixed forgetting wins.
- SIGN_REVERSAL vs CONSTANT_FORGETTING: **+82.67e−6** ([+53.85e−6,+115.72e−6]). Simple fixed forgetting wins.
- RAPIDLY_VARIABLE vs CONSTANT_FORGETTING: **+8.82e−6** ([−6.21e−6,+23.89e−6]), inconclusive.
- Against FROZEN_AFTER_CALIBRATION, adaptive is better in every tested regime, which is expected because continued updates are useful when initial calibration remains noisy or transport changes. This does not by itself justify the more elaborate trigger.

Curvature-entry estimation error and coefficient error were recorded independently. They need not agree: different parameter pairs can produce a similar product a*b and thus similar curvature while defining different parallel transports. Curvature error alone is NOT a sufficient proxy for causal transport error.

### Stability and scope

The local exponential is computed in closed form (hyperbolic boost), avoiding unstable long power-series summation for this bounded domain. RLS variances remain positive and coefficients are bounded; tests check finite derivatives, neutral metric and bounded numerics. These constraints prevent obvious numerical disasters, but parameter clipping itself can bias the estimator on unmodeled worlds.

No true metric evolution or change of bundle topology occurs: **the connection adapts inside a fixed metric-compatible structural family**. The nontrivial global twist is inherited and tested, not learned. The simulator's input test sections and chart maps are privileged; self-organized neurite growth, inter-organ exchange, adaptive algebra selection, semantic concepts, energy-efficient hardware and cognition remain untested.

## Verdict and next frontier

**M:** A mathematically constrained, time-varying rank-four neutral bundle connection can be learned from partial test-section observations and retain local chart, curvature, metric and winding compatibility.

**R/H:** The surprise-triggered adaptive q rule adds complexity that is not presently justified against a simple constant-forgetting controller in fast-changing worlds. Do not tune it on seeds100..131 and present those seeds as an independent result again.

**Next:** Couple this adaptive rank-four connection to the G0-AB organism *without giving it privileged calibration sections or known source charts*, and test online endogenous chart inference, typed remainder and causal consequences against compute-/information-matched conventional controllers. Require a newly frozen seed range, actual energy measurement for efficiency claims, and explicit failure detection if curvature/transport drift under dissipation.
