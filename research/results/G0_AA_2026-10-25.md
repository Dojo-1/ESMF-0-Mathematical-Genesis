# G0-AA · Change-point repair and temporal Worldlet split — 2026-10-25

Protocol: [G0_AA_TEMPORAL_REPAIR_PROTOCOL.md](../G0_AA_TEMPORAL_REPAIR_PROTOCOL.md), committed before first experiment. Source `esmf/temporal_repair_aa.py`, tests `tests/test_temporal_repair_aa.py`.

## Mathematical model

13 competing weighted Gaussian hypotheses:
- two persistent full-history latent states with T=identity/transpose (total prior .45);
- ten hypotheses with one boundary K∈{1..5} and two independent Gaussian state segments, retaining T across the boundary (total prior .35);
- one diffuse model with a new latent state at every observation (prior .20).

On each scalar observation of sign(T,parity)*D_segment plus Gaussian noise, hypotheses update exact predictive marginal likelihood and conjugate latent state posteriors. The inferred cut K is not supplied to the filter.

**Typed inference repair:** if posterior P(split)≥.50 and max_K P(K|split)≥.30, emit descriptors for two temporal Worldlets with span [0,K], [K,6], a posterior boundary probability and [R]_id. This is a conditional representation split, NOT tissue growth, actual memory consolidation, proven stack descent or a learned arbitrary topology.

The same frozen six-sensor schedule and energy budget from G0-Z are used. The stimulus generator is independently replay-tested against G0-Z observations/posteriors for shared/independent and change-identity worlds.

## Interpretation and open obligations

The local identity hypothesis must admit (1) persistent source, (2) segmented-but-meaningfully-related source, and (3) diffuse replacement. The model distinguishes these sufficiently to recover some legitimate orientation claims after hidden replacements; false segmentation and resource overhead remain significant.

**Data-generating bias:** The three worlds match the finite hypothesis classes unusually well. Strong controls still needed: gradually drifting D, two or more resets, non-Gaussian noise, correlation between measurement noise and hidden T, changing sensor calibrations, unknown K under different action schedules, and strong conventional state-space baselines with equally flexible model families.

**No complete ESMF organism exists yet.** The physical/biochemical protomap, event-driven electrochemistry, polyvisceral loops and true heterogeneous bundle atlas are NOT integrated into this hidden-contrast filtering module. The connection to the neutral bundle is via one scalar, orientation-sensitive quotient; that is a feature-limited instrument, not a faithful full-bundle implementation. Truthful evidence: [P] conditional Gaussian algebra; [A] candidate model/priors/energy; [M] seed-level numerical outcomes; [H] semantic identity or biological/energy advantages.

## Design implication

An organism should preserve `P(orientation|continuity)`, `P(continuity class)`, and `P(change boundary|segmented)` separately. The energy-intensive auditor need not run continuously: a compiled fast local probe protocol can service ordinary contexts, while posterior-predictive surprises or change candidates trigger slower context repair. This is a proposed next experiment, not already implemented.
