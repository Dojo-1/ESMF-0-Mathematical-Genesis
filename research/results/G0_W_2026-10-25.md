## Obstruction and construction

The causal action in G0-U induces LEFT multiplication on the four-dimensional fiber Mat₂(R). For any A∈GL₂(R), its induced real 4×4 matrix L_A satisfies det L_A=(det A)²>0. Therefore any fixed-rank bundle whose transition structure comes **only** from this left GL₂ action is orientable. It cannot directly instantiate G0-Q's nonorientable real rank-four bundle.

Define tau(F)=F^T. Its 4×4 representation P swaps entries b and c, has det P=-1, and preserves the determinant quadratic form Q(F)=det F of signature (2,2): P^T H P=H. Hence tau supplies a genuinely orientation-reversing neutral isometry usable as monodromy across a base S¹ loop.

For A,B∈SL₂(R), independent factor actions F↦A F B^T preserve Q. Left actions and right actions commute with *each other*, but action order within either SL₂ factor need not commute. The transpose twist interchanges the factors:
tau(A F B^T)=B tau(F) A^T.

Accordingly, the structure suggested by these operators is the semidirect product (SL₂(R)×SL₂(R))⋊Z₂ with the Z₂ generator swapping the two factors. The representation can have a finite kernel; this is an **operator-family design**, not a claim that every component of O(2,2) or its spin double cover has been implemented.

Crucial seam law: P L_A P^{-1}=R_{A^T}. A locally described dynamics that applies L_A uniformly across a transpose seam violates the bundle gluing; it must switch to its conjugated RIGHT action. Thus crossing the seam exchanges two possible semi-autonomous regulation channels without erasing the neutral invariant.

## What survives, and what does not

**P (conditional elementary algebra)**: det(L_A) square, signature of determinant, transpose orientation-reversing isometry, left/right conjugation identity, orientation obstruction for left-action-only transition structure.

**M (sampled numerical)**: listed checks and failure of the chart-blind ablation.

**H**: benefit from two independently developing regulatory organs whose action regimes exchange across contexts; benefits of topological winding in causal prediction, memory or computation.

**NOT claimed:** the G0-S/T learner already learns the seam monodromy; nontrivial topology improves prediction or causes intelligence; physical cortex/viscera are literally SL₂ systems; transpose is a biological operator; full bundle connection has been learned. ## New falsifiable hypothesis

A dual-factor surrogate with fast sensorimotor updates on one SL₂ channel and slower regulatory/plasticity updates on the other may retain useful context when representations exchange roles. To test, make the seam/context switch hidden and infer it from observations; compare against (1) equally informed conventional recurrent control, (2) an order-aware no-twist control, and (3) a fully calibrated chart-aware real baseline. Require matched parameters, calibration exposures, wall time and independently measured resource cost before promoting any advantage.
