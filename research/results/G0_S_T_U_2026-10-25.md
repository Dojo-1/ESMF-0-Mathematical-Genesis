# G0-S/T/U · First measured bridge: causal learning, chart gluing, neutral bundle transport
Date: 2026-10-25

## Question

Can an independently simulated intervention system be learned from short action/sensor traces, generalized to longer unseen paths, and transported across independently calibrated local charts while maintaining a real neutral signature (2,2) bilinear invariant?

The claim tested is **implementation of causal state learning with consistent geometric transport**, not a unique advantage of neutral geometry over equivalent real recurrences.

**Pre-registered S protocol:** [G0_S_CAUSAL_PROTOCOL.md](../G0_S_CAUSAL_PROTOCOL.md), committed before the first experiment execution.

## Mathematical object

Body-environment base M=S^1×R, state (q,p). Actions:
X_d(q,p)=(q+a d tanh p,p) and Y_d(q,p)=(q,p+b d sin q).
Both are reversible and preserve dq∧dp: det DX=det DY=1. Their vector fields have generally nonzero Lie bracket, which is sampled by a closed four-action loop. This is NOT by itself a proof of nonzero connection curvature.

Tangent frame F∈Mat₂(R) transforms by F' = J F for the action Jacobian J∈SL₂(R). The four-dimensional associated fiber carries Q(F)=det F=(u₁²+u₂²−v₁²−v₂²), signature (2,2). The induced GL₄ action L_J preserves its bilinear polarization H. A general local sensor chart with Jacobian B changes fiber coordinates by L_B and changes the fiber metric by H_B=L_B^{-T}HL_B^{-1}. Hence P_{a→b}^T H_b P_{a→b}=H_a when the physical action is area-preserving. Positive auxiliary description cost is similarly chart-covariant but is *not conserved in time*.

This associated-bundle instance is topologically trivial over the cylinder; it does **not** automatically inherit G0-Q's separate nonorientable twist. Physical dissipative transitions violate this neutral isometry, as they should.

## Experimental protocol and severe limitations

Eight paired seeds were used initially and thirty-two later as an exploratory sensitivity run. Each seed changes the two true action coefficients and the sampled episodes. Models see 140 noisy length-1–3 training episodes and are tested on 220 unseen length-4–8 episodes and 120 intervention commutator loops. All three real-state models have **two learnable physical coefficients** with the same full-batch optimizer and epoch budget.

The NONLINEAR model's action family **exactly matches the data generator**; superior extrapolation is therefore expected for this synthetic family. The FLAT commuting translation environment is the prespecified negative-world control and reverses the ranking.

## G0-T: locally learned sensor charts

Three nonlinear local views of the same state:
- chart 0: (q,p);
- chart 1: (q+κ tanh(p),sinh(p));
- chart 2: (q+λ sin(p),2p).

Each seed independently estimates κ and λ from 32 noisy, unlabeled paired calibration sightings. Beginning and ending sensor charts vary independently during training and evaluation. Exact cocycle reconstruction, Jacobian-chart transformation and tangent/neutral covariance are separately checked.

| Noncommuting environment, long paths | Mean held-out loss |
|---|---:|
| Correct calibration parameters (oracle) | 0.000005071 |

Chart-aware wins against chart-blind in 32/32 seeds. Paired mean gap = 0.05613238, 95% seed bootstrap [0.05209149,0.06053916]. Max absolute coefficient calibration error across 32 seeds was 0.004080. Loops: calibrated mean 4.55e-7 vs chart-blind 0.03830.

**Fair baseline boundary:** a conventional sequential real predictor given the *same* calibration functions also matches calibrated model predictions. Thus chart-blind is an ablation of essential calibration, not evidence that bundle architecture beats the strongest coordinate-aware conventional model. Extra chart-decoding work has not been offset by any measured energy saving.

## What is NOT established

- No conceptual/semantic abstraction, no acquired inter-organs, no living tissue morphogenesis, no learned fiber topological class, no polyholonomic identity.
- The exact-match nonlinear generative family strongly favors NONLINEAR. It is standard state-space learning with a geometric tangent companion. No measured advantage of *bundle representation itself* over an equally calibrated ordinary recurrent model.
- No formal Lean theorem of the entire learning algorithm, no independent third-party replication, no measured Watts/energy, no estimate of scalability or actual hardware efficiency.
- Chart calibrations receive paired sensor sightings (extra information that must be counted).
- The bundle topological twist from G0-Q has not yet been coupled into the causal learner.
- Synthetic world family, action distribution and horizon are narrow. Bootstrapped intervals are conditional on these choices.

**Evidence:** P for elementary determinant and Jacobian algebra under specified assumptions; A for synthetic dynamics and linearization; M for cited bounded checks; H for cognitive and efficiency benefit. Preserve the weak/null result against an equally informed real recurrence as an active constraint on future claims.

## Next discriminating experiment

Move from exact-match 2-parameter toy dynamics to partial sensory access, out-of-family interventions and independently adapted local organs. Compare bundle-constrained and non-geometric recurrent models *both calibrated and compute-matched*. Require gain under coordinate drift not removable by ordinary preprocessing, actual task transfer, and measured CPU/GPU energy before claiming practical efficiency. Test global winding twist and curvature only if they induce a falsifiable change in behavior; random extra geometry is not an advantage.
