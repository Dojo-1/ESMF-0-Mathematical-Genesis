## Mathematical corollary [P conditional]

Two even observations cannot identify hidden T but can identify C∈{shared versus independent D}: under shared D, Cov(Y₁,Y₂)=Var(D)>0, under independently redrawn D, covariance=0, while the marginals coincide. This changes the question that the measurement answers.

Under H_shared, both orientation submodels H_I and H_P reproduce exactly G0-Y's conditional posterior. The additional H_ind component changes whether that conditional answer is *authorized*, not its conditional likelihood calculation. Tests verify this noninterference. Evidence for identity continuity should not be conflated with evidence for orientation.
