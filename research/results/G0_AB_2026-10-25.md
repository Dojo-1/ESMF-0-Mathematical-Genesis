# G0-AB · Adaptive organ ecology — held-out experiment, 2026-10-25

## Executive scientific finding

**Multi-clock adaptation is implemented and bounded, but it has not demonstrated better held-out causal prediction than a fixed focused-sensing circuit.** Slow plasticity and context budding are causal mechanisms in the code, not just annotations. They did not yield a sustained utility gain in this synthetic regime. The negative comparison remains active and should constrain later claims.

## What concretely adapts [A / tested M]

### Carrier and inference

The latent body-fiber state follows F_t=[[1,D_t],[0,1]]∈SL₂(R). Its determinant is 1, an invariant of the four-dimensional Mat₂(R) neutral quadratic form Q(F)=det F of signature (2,2); orientation transposition preserves Q. **Crucial:** F_t lies on a **one-dimensional unipotent orbit** inside the four-dimensional carrier. The experiment therefore does not demonstrate useful four-dimensional cognitive complexity or physically faithful cortical tissue.

Observations are only scalar projections of the hidden antisymmetric contrast D_t. The filter maintains:
- two candidate orientation classes T∈{identity,transpose};
- three local continuation modes: nearly stable, drifting AR process, newly reset latent state;
- a separate no-continuity hypothesis H_diffuse under which D_t is independently replaced on every event.

The Gaussian interacting-multiple-model (IMM) filter uses moment-matched local transports. Those are approximations with [R]_transport, not exact full sequence Bayesian filtering. A claim of orientation requires **joint posterior** P(persistent source,T=t)≥.95. The same exact algorithm expressed as an ordinary real state-space model yields the SAME predictions as the neutral-bundle interpretation: no uniquely geometric performance credit.

### Distinct clocks and actual behavioral changes

- **FAST (12 ticks per episode):** choose parity-dependent probe, receive noisy partial observation, update seven-component state mixture; external source D_t evolves.
- **MESO (every two ticks):** surprise- and continuity-sensitive quiet/focus sensing with hysteresis, resource admission and economical continuity rechecks under suspected source nonpersistence.
- **SLOW (every four ticks):** feedback updates the future reset hazard h∈[.005,.30], using only evidence already received. High but sufficiently source-grounded reset evidence can create a typed `[R]_id` boundary descriptor, preserve conditional orientation and reweight future model-mode transitions. A refractory period prevents immediate repeated budding.
- **Homeostatic ledger:** reserve initial 8, each tick supplies up to .16 external resource, explicit overflow, sensor/maintenance expenditure and viability floor .25. The gross spend can exceed 8 because the organism receives external supply during the episode; ledger closure is tested.

These are useful **surrogate control organs**, not demonstrated spinal/autonomic/enteric biological systems. No learned morphogenetic tissue, electrochemical ion dynamics, distributed neural cognition, or actual joule measurements exist in G0-AB.

| SWITCH (stable→drift) | .60938 | .83594 | .05741 | .03689 | 8.8054 | 8.9900 |
| DIFFUSE (unidentifiable T) | .02344 unsupported | .03125 unsupported | N/A | N/A | 8.3606 | 8.9900 |

Coverage is the fraction making any ≥.95 joint-source-and-orientation decision. The fixed-focused circuit occasionally makes one incorrect orientation declaration within 128 trials in each of the four meaningful-T worlds; the adaptive model makes none at that threshold but abstains more often. Hence *selective accuracy* alone misleads without coverage and calibration.

DIFFUSE has **no meaningful persistent T to infer**, even though the simulator can sample a hidden bit. Any confident statement that one global orientation persists across the fully independent latent states is unsupported. The revised adaptive system STILL committed 3/128 such declarations; fixed focused made 4/128 and fixed quiet none. This calibration failure remains open.

### Paired uncertainty and budget tradeoffs

Differences are ADAPTIVE − FIXED_FOCUS, using 64 seed clusters (two orientations each) and deterministic 2,000-sample bootstrap intervals:
- STABLE correct-declarations per episode: −.18750, 95% bootstrap [−.28125,−.10156]; resource −.21414 [−.34453,−.08477].
- DRIFT mean Brier +.03264 [.00782,.06002], and resource −.14555 [−.26313,−.03008].
- JUMPS correct-declarations per episode −.20313 [−.31250,−.10156], resource +.06641 [−.01195,.13578]. The extra model plasticity is not free and shows no robust saving here.
- SWITCH correct-declarations per episode −.21875 [−.32031,−.13281]; resource −.18461 [−.29844,−.07586].
- DIFFUSE abstract resource −.62938 [−.77352,−.50047], but 3/128 unsupported orientation declarations remain. Conserving resources does not establish successful epistemic calibration.

Intervals characterize this narrow synthetic seed distribution; they are not confidence intervals on biological systems or deployment performance.

### Actual local structural adaptation

On JUMPS, adaptive emitted 0.3828 temporal context buds per episode (49 events /128 episodes) and detected 19.14% of the 256 evaluator-known reset events within ±1 tick (49 /256). In this toy world these emissions aligned with one of the two true reset windows; **recall is low**. On STABLE the held-out bud count was zero; on DRIFT 0.0313/episode, SWITCH 0.0391/episode and DIFFUSE 0.0156/episode, representing non-target or false buds under those evaluator regimes. Structural adaptation changes future regime probabilities and carries a `[R]_id` marker; it is not a grown biological organ.

`adaptive_no_slow` makes no buds and holds the reset-hazard prior constant. In JUMPS, it achieved coverage .59375 versus .56250 with slow plasticity, and Brier .01259 versus .01312. The paired Brier difference adaptive−no_slow was +.000528 [+.000181,+.000981]. **In this test the slow adaptation made prediction marginally worse.** Other environments show no convincing gain attributable to slow plasticity.

## Honest conclusions and next decision

1. The architecture now **adapts causally** at sensing, mode-transition and context-memory levels [M for demonstrated behavior], including a real multi-clock update schedule and bounded resource ledger.
2. It does NOT win the primary predictive comparison against a well-chosen simple circuit. `fixed_focus` is presently a stronger predictor; `fixed_quiet` is cheaper but less informative.
3. Slow feedback is not currently justified by predictive benefit, and false-source-confidence is not eliminated.
4. The body/geometry is still a **partial scalar quotient** of a correctly neutral four-dimensional carrier; no full sensorimotor world, tissue morphogenesis, spatial connection learning or electrochemical hardware has been shown.
5. Next G0-AC should compare a **cheap reflex with triggered expensive audit**, and adapt the *policy itself* only upon held-out predictive surprises. It must demonstrate a Pareto improvement against fixed focus at comparable resource and correct-decision quality, with real measured energy if an efficiency claim is made. A new protocol and fresh held-out seeds are required before changing these postpilot rules.

Evidence: [P] for determinant and source-conditional mathematical identities; [A] surrogate laws and priors; [M] finite remote runs and controls; [H] cognitive/semantic/biological efficiency; [R]_transport and [R]_id remain distinct.
